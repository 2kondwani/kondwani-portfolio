About

Kondwani Phiri

Hi! I’m Kondwani, I’m a Mechanical Engineering & Cinematic Arts student at the University of Southern California.

I’ve been interested in both filmmaking and engineering since I was young. For engineering roles, I’m really interested in product design, aerospace, consumer hardware, and biotechnology. In cinema, I primarily write, direct, and edit, and I’m also actively seeking opportunities to grow, either in post-production or with camera teams (to develop as a cinematographer/DP).

As a hobby, I’ve grown to love film photography, with all my personal work on here. Anyone interested in collaborating or a role that might be a fit can feel free to contact me directly.

I grew up in Silver Spring, Maryland, just outside of Washington, DC. I live between there and Los Angeles for school, and have deep ties to New York City because I spent a portion of my childhood there also. Long story short, if you need me in the DMV, LA, or New York, I can make it work. As a matter of fact, just about anywhere works.
